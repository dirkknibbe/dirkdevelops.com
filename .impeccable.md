## Design Context

### Users
- **Hiring managers / recruiters** scanning candidates — need to quickly see credibility and competence
- **Fellow engineers** checking out work — want to see real technical depth, not fluff
- **Potential collaborators** — want to understand what Dirk builds and how to reach him
- Context: visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, or resume links. First impression matters. Most will spend 30-90 seconds.

### Brand Personality
**Sharp, inventive, technical.**

- Confident without being arrogant
- Technical depth shown through the work, not decoration
- Craft-conscious — every detail is intentional
- An engineer who ships, not a designer who codes

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Hybrid of Rauno Freiberg (quiet precision) and Elliott Mangham (editorial cinematic)**
- Near-black palette with zinc tones (#09090b base)
- Subtle grid texture background
- Numbered editorial sections (01, 02, 03, 04) with accent lines
- Dot nav for scroll progress
- Generous whitespace, restrained typography
- Serif accents (Playfair Display italic) for emphasis within sans-serif body (Inter)
- Pretext.js for hero name animation (fluid character-level physics) and subtle scroll-triggered text touches
- Single continuous scroll with cinematic pacing, distinct routes for deep-dive content

### Anti-references
- Generic AI-generated templates (cyan-on-dark, purple gradients, glassmorphism)
- Cutesy illustrations or emoji-heavy copy
- Card grids with icons above every heading
- Anything that looks like a Vercel/Next.js starter template

### Design Principles
1. **Quiet confidence** — let the work speak; decoration earns its place or gets cut
2. **Cinematic pacing** — each section is a scene; scroll reveals create rhythm, not noise
3. **Technical authenticity** — the site itself demonstrates engineering craft (Pretext, Astro, performance)
4. **Progressive disclosure** — hook with the hero, reward with depth on sub-pages
5. **Every pixel intentional** — if you can't explain why it's there, remove it
